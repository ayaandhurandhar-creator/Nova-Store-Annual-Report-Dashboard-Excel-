## Key Insights
- Women customers contributed approximately 65% of total purchases.
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were the top-performing states contributing around 35% of sales.
- Adult age group (30–49 years) contributed nearly 50% of total sales.
- Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra were the highest contributing sales channels with around 80% contribution.

--------




## Conclusion
To improve sales, Nova Store should focus on women customers aged 30–49 years living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh through targeted ads, offers, and coupons on Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
